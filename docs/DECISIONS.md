# Decisions

Every judgement call, with the quoted rule text where one exists. Precedence when sources
disagree: Wahapedia (reflects the online update log, June 2026) > printed text.

| id | Decision | Rationale / source | Owner confirmation? |

| D-001 | **Stack: TypeScript + Vite + Preact, SVG board.** | Owner decision on TS+Vite. Preact over React: same API, ~4kB runtime, matters on a phone. SVG over canvas: crisp at any zoom, free hit-testing via DOM events, and terrain is a few hundred polygons — well within SVG's comfort zone. A DPR-aware canvas remains an option if profiling on a low-end phone shows a problem. | no |
| D-002 | **Close Quarters rules apply to Gallowdark AND Tomb World.** | The Killzones page says "Close Quarters — *Killzone: Gallowdark* has the following additional rules"; Tomb World's own section only says its rules are kept consistent with Gallowdark, and the Tomb World box rules include them. Owner decided both. Gated by `map.closeQuarters`, never by killzone id. | decided by owner — do not change |
| D-003 | **Guard is NOT universal.** | Guard is a Close Quarters universal action; core rules only reference it in Counteract ("excluding Guard"). The previous app offered it on every map — that was wrong. If the June 2026 update log makes it universal, flip `closeQuarters` gating, not the action. | flag if the update log says otherwise |
| D-004 | **The 6 Non-specific killzone maps are out of scope.** | Owner decision; they contain no terrain. | no |
| D-005 | **Model heights by base class** (`MODEL_HEIGHT_BY_BASE` in `src/core/state.ts`): 25mm 1.3", 28mm 1.35", 32mm 1.6", 40mm 1.9", 50mm/60×35 2.2", 75×42 2.5". | Visibility is drawn from the miniature's **head**, so a height is required. These are estimates from standard GW model proportions and are data, not hard-coded rules — a datacard may override via `Datacard.height`. | **yes — please sanity-check against your models** |
| D-006 | **Visibility sampling**: head point → 4 rings × 10 perimeter samples + top centre on the target's cylinder, target radius = 0.8× base radius, lowest ring lifted 0.12" so a line grazing the floor does not count. Bases never block ("Ignore operatives' bases when determining this"). | Core rules › Visible. A sampled silhouette is the standard approximation for a 1mm line to "any part of the miniature". | no |
| D-007 | **Targeting lines**: 12 perimeter samples on the shooter's base × the facing half of the target's base; 2D when heights match (<0.05"), 3D otherwise. | Core rules › Intervening: "from any point of its base to every facing part of the intended target's base... if there's a difference in height... three-dimensional". | no |
| D-008 | **Wall corners/ends** are modelled as 0.35"-radius zones at polygon vertices whose adjacent edges are ≥0.6" and turn by more than ~32°. | "only the corners and ends of Wall terrain can intervene... minor parts of the wall that protrude do not make a corner or end alone; it must be the main structure of the wall that turns a corner or ends." | no |
| D-009 | **"The active operative has passed it"** is modelled as the corner lying behind the shooter along the shooter→target axis. | Killzones › Wall terrain, with the printed diagram ("if operative A was wholly on the left side of the dotted black line…"). | no |
| D-010 | **Undo is limited to uncommitted input** plus the rules' own cancel-and-revert ("If an action is declared or begun but it's not possible to complete, the action is cancelled. Revert back to the game state before that action"). Once dice are rolled there is no undo. An optional house-rule undo may be added, off by default and logged in the replay. | The previous app's deep-snapshot undo is not the rules. | no |
| D-011 | **Devastating x is applied on retained crits before blocking**, and the success is still resolved afterwards. | "Each retained critical success immediately inflicts x damage... Note that success isn't discarded after doing so — it can still be resolved later in the sequence." | no |
| D-012 | **Default cover-vs-obscured choice for auto-resolution is obscured.** | Obscured costs the attacker a success *and* removes every crit; a cover save is one die. Applies only to auto/AI resolution — a human is always asked. | no |
| D-013 | **Objective markers are always on the killzone floor, except in Killzone: Bheta-Decima.** The extractor forces `z = 0` for every objective outside Bheta-Decima, whatever terrain footprint the marker is printed inside; `onFeatureId` is still recorded. Bheta-Decima keeps its printed heights (the condenser roof in maps 1-3, gantries elsewhere). | Appendix › GAME SEQUENCE: "Other than in Killzone: Bheta-Decima, all objective markers must be set up on the killzone floor." Owner confirmed 2026-08-17 ("ALL OBJECTIVES MUST BE ON THE KILLZONE FLOOR"), keeping the Bheta exception the brief documents. Three Volkus markers are printed inside a structure's footprint (volkus-4 P1 in Stronghold A, volkus-5 P2 and volkus-6 centre in large ruin C) — those are on the ground floor **under** the Ceiling/Vantage upper level, which is exactly what Ceiling terrain means. Pinned by `tests/integration.test.ts`. | confirmed by owner |
| D-014 | **A dotted/dashed outline on a map card means the piece goes slightly UNDER the adjacent raised terrain; a solid outline means it sits on top.** Applies to terrain pieces. The dashed rubble pieces inside Volkus strongholds and large ruins (G, I, J, K) are therefore on the killzone floor beneath the upper level, at `z0 = 0`. | Owner ruling 2026-08-17. This **overrides the brief's inference**, which read a white dashed rectangular outline inside a stronghold or large ruin as "a rubble piece placed on that structure's upper level" and asked for confirmation. The extracted data already had these pieces at `z0 = 0`, so no regeneration was needed. | confirmed by owner |
| D-015 | **"…can immediately perform a free 1AP action" is modelled as one extra AP restricted to the named action** (`grantFreeAction` in `src/teams/helpers.ts`), landing on that operative's next activation when the grant is made outside one. | The engine has no intent for performing an action outside an activation, and `canPerformAction` can only forbid, never permit. The bonus AP is always the last one the operative spends, so `canPerformAction` refuses anything outside the rule's named actions (and any move action when the rule says "it cannot move during that action") once it is being spent. Consequence: an operative already at −1 APL gets nothing, because APL changes clamp to ±1. Affects Kasrkin SEIZE THE INITIATIVE / RELOCATE / COVER RETREAT and Angels of Death WRATH OF VENGEANCE. | no |
| D-016 | **A ploy that needs a position or a target takes it from the intent's `data`; with none supplied it uses a deterministic, logged default** (the centroid of the friendly operatives the rule names, or the lowest-id eligible operative). | `UseGambit`/`UsePloy` carry a `data` payload but no decision channel, and a killzone position is continuous so it cannot be enumerated as `DecisionOption`s. The UI always passes `data`; the default exists so a bot-vs-bot game never stalls, and it is written to the log. Affects Kasrkin CLEARANCE SWEEP, Imperial Navy Breachers ATTACK/DEFENCE ORDER, Plague Marines CLOUD OF FLIES / NURGLINGS / VIRULENT POISON. | no |
| D-017 | **Angels of Death CHAPTER TACTICS are chosen through the pure setter `setChapterTactics(state, player, primary, secondary)`, and nothing applies until they are.** | "When selecting your kill team, select a primary and secondary CHAPTER TACTIC" — the Select Operatives step has no decision channel in the engine, and picking one silently would be worse than picking none. The same applies to CHAPTER VETERAN (`setVeteranTactic`). | flag if a Select Operatives decision channel lands | 
| D-018 | **"Once per turning point … if you roll two or more fails, you can discard one of them to retain another as a normal success" is offered as a `mode: 'fails'` re-roll grant.** | The retention step (`retentionOptions` in `dice.ts`) only knows Severe/Rending/Punishing and `onAttackDiceRetained` is declared but never emitted, so there is no seam for a team-specific retention transform. A single-fail re-roll is the closest the dice engine's decision vocabulary gets: it costs the same die and is offered to the same player at the same moment, but a re-roll can fail where the printed rule cannot. Affects Kasrkin RELICS OF CADIA, Angels of Death PURITY SEALS / INDOMITUS, Imperial Navy Breachers CLOSE ASSAULT. | **yes — worth an `onAttackDiceRetained` emit so this can be exact** |
| D-019 | **A weapon's "+1 to both Dmg stats" is applied where the damage is inflicted (`onDamage`), not by rewriting the profile.** | `WeaponProfile` objects come from the datacard catalogue and are shared by every operative in every battle; mutating one would leak across games (architecture rule 7). Affects the rare `Toxic` rule and Imperial Navy Breachers CLOSE ASSAULT. | no |
| D-020 | **A team rule that the hook surface cannot express gets a small additive core seam rather than being left a no-op.** Batch 1's second half added: `onSelectTarget` (target substitution) and `onStunTest` (new hooks, both emitted); `onValidTarget.viewFrom` + `ignoreFriendlyControlRange` (payload fields); the previously-dead `onValidTarget.forceVisible` honoured; and `onSelectWeapon` actually emitted. | CLAUDE.md architecture rule 5: "an unknown hook name is a build error … never a silent no-op" — a *declared but unemitted* hook is exactly the silent no-op that rule forbids, and four printed rules (HOLY DEFENDER, SAVIOUR PROTOCOLS, Magnify, PSYK-OUT GRENADES) have no other honest expression. Every seam is listed in docs/TEAM-STATUS.md § "Engine seams added for team rules". | no |
| D-021 | **A rule that grants an action the universal one forbids is registered as its own `ActionDef` with `treatedAs` pointing at the universal action.** Kommandos `Charge (Throat Slittas)` (Conceal), `Shoot (Explosive)` (while engaged, self-targeting), `Fight (Krumpin' Time)`, `Smoke/Stun Grenade (Taktical Wot-notz)`. | `canPerformAction` can only forbid, never permit, and the universal action's own `check` is the blocker. `treatedAs` keeps action restrictions shared, so the carve-out can never be used twice. Follows the Kasrkin `Shoot (Rapid Fire)` precedent. Consequence: `src/ai/legal.ts` only builds movement params for the four universal move ids, so the AI never uses `Charge (Throat Slittas)`; a human can. | no |
| D-022 | **"You can use this rule" is auto-used on a stated, deterministic policy when it is free, and raised as a `PendingDecision` when it costs something.** AUTO-FLAGELLATOR (self-damage) asks; SAINTLY RELICS fires only against damage of 3+ or a killing blow; HOLY DEFENDER redirects only when the ABJUROR has more wounds left than the operative it shields; Boom! always rolls two D6 because the second is free. | The engine cannot ask a question inside `onDamage` without stalling a dice sequence, and a decision on every attack dice would drown the log. Each policy is written next to the rule it governs and logged when it fires. | no |
| D-023 | **Reanimation Protocols' Ready-step roll is a real decision** (which marker, and the order the operative comes back with), and an operative REANIMATED during that step still gets its Living Metal wounds. | "select one of your Reanimation markers and roll one D6" is a player choice, and Living Metal resolves "after resolving all other rules in this step (e.g. Reanimation Protocols)". A REANIMATED operative keeps its operative id, so the Martyrs tac op's per-victim ledger cannot score it twice — pinned by a test in `tests/teams/hierotek-circle.test.ts`. | no |
| D-024 | **Kommandos' Boom! resolves as direct Blast damage instead of a free Shoot action.** The 2D6 is rolled as printed; on a 4+ each operative within the explosives' Blast radius takes its Normal Dmg, with no defence roll. | "this operative performs a free Shoot action with its explosives before it's removed from the killzone" happens inside `inflictDamage`, i.e. inside another in-flight shoot/fight sequence — starting a second sequence there would clobber the first (`state.sequence` is single-slot), and `onIncapacitated.freeActions` is declared but never consumed. Flagged in docs/TEAM-STATUS.md; the honest fix is a queued free-action seam. | **yes — worth a queued "free action" seam so death triggers can shoot properly** |
| D-025 | **Anti-PSYKER is implemented as the Celestian Insidiants' own printed footnote: "it has the Lethal 5+ weapon rule".** `data/teams/_rare-weapon-rules.json` defines the shared rule as "add 1 to both Dmg stats … and it has the Lethal 5+ weapon rule", sourced from the Novitiates' datacard. | Precedence is the team's own printed text for its own weapons (the rule is footnoted on each Celestian datacard). The discrepancy is real and belongs to whoever implements the Novitiates: either the two teams print different versions, or the shared definition is wrong for one of them. | **yes — please check a Celestian Insidiants card against a Novitiates one** |
| D-026 | **A unique action's whole legality — target, range, visibility, once-per-turning-point — lives in `check`; `perform` may only fail for reasons `check` already excluded.** | `src/ai/legal.ts` `missionCandidates` tries plausible params (including friendly targets) and offers anything whose `check` passes; a `perform` that then refuses becomes a REJECTED INTENT, which the soak bar forbids. Found the hard way: the first Celestian soak rejected SPEAK OF HER DEEDS, and the same shape is still live in Plague Marines' POISONOUS MIASMA. | no |
| D-027 | **Bheta-Decima gantry deck height = 3.0"**, and the condenser roof with it. | No published measurement exists for the Bheta-Decima gantries. 3.0" matches the Volkus stronghold first floor, sits inside the 3" climb reach from the killzone floor and above the 2" free-drop threshold. **Owner confirmed 2026-08-17** ("3 inches is a fine assumption"); `data/terrain/bheta-decima.json` now records `owner-confirmed` rather than `assumed`. | confirmed by owner |
| D-028 | **`onCounteract` is emitted before the Engage-order test, which becomes the event's default rather than a pre-filter.** `counteractCandidates` now emits for every expended, not-yet-counteracted operative with `allowed: o.order === 'engage'`, so a team rule may widen it; `whoActivates` gained an optional `ctx` and consults `counteractCandidates` when given one. | "This operative can counteract regardless of its order" is printed on the Astartes faction rule of **twelve** kill teams (ten implemented). Filtering before the emit meant the hook could only ever narrow the list the core had already computed, so every one of those clauses was unreachable — and batch 1 shipped Angels of Death and Plague Marines with the handler registered but inert, which is precisely the silent no-op CLAUDE.md rule 5 forbids. `whoActivates` had to move with it or the candidate list and the turn test would disagree; as a side effect the two now also agree about the On Guard lockout, which `whoActivates` did not check. Costs one line of `src/ai/legal.ts` (passing the ctx it already holds). | no |
| D-029 | **A printed `maxCount` whose role matches no selection-list row falls back to a datacard KEYWORD match** (`src/teams/selection.ts`). | The Deathwatch print "your kill team can only include up to one GRAVIS operative" scrapes as `{kind:'maxCount', role:'GRAVIS'}`, but GRAVIS is a keyword shared by the BOMBARD, BREACHER and HORDE-SLAYER rows rather than a selection role. Matching `entry.role` only made the constraint dead, and `defaultRoster` produced a two-GRAVIS kill team that `validateRosterFor` then accepted. Extending the vocabulary in the shared validator is the fix CLAUDE.md asks for; a per-team `if` is not. | no |
| D-030 | **`maxItem` selection constraints stay unenforced for now**, and the roster builder keeps surfacing them under "⚠ N rules this app does not check". | Enforcing them is a two-line count over the weapons `validateRosterFor` already resolves — but `defaultRoster` always picks `loadouts[0]`, so a capped weapon that several rows offer first makes the team unfillable: turning the check on left `void-dancer-troupe` able to field only 2 of its 7 operatives. The honest fix is a loadout-aware `defaultRoster` that backtracks to another option, which is its own change. Affects warpcoven, canoptek-circle, hunter-clade, void-dancer-troupe, hand-of-the-archon, sanctifiers, xv26 and death-korps. | no |
| D-031 | **`onRollAttack` is still NOT emitted by `fight.ts`, deliberately.** Six of the eight batch-2 teams need it and each says so in its notes column. | Every melee re-roll rule in the game is unreachable without it, so it is the highest-value seam left — but 15 of the 17 existing `onRollAttack` handlers across batches 1 and 2 do not guard on `ev.ctx.type`. Emitting it in fights would silently change melee behaviour for all of them: some print "shooting, fighting or retaliating" and would become more correct, others are shooting-only and would start firing where they should not. It needs its own change with all 17 handlers audited, not a blind add inside a teams PR. | **yes — worth scheduling; it unblocks Designate Target, Cast the Runes, CHAOS TALISMANS, Screecher, RAVAGE DESTINY, FATE ITSELF IS MY WEAPON and PURITY SEALS** |
